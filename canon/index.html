The Canon Behind Every Page

74 principles from behavioural science, interface research, the copywriting legends and current search guidance. Each links to the samples that use it, so you can see the idea working on a real page. A few apply to every sample and are marked that way.

The Ethics Test

Use these to help a person make a decision they will be glad they made. If a tactic only works because the person did not see it clearly, it is not in this library. That is why you will find no fake countdown timers, no invented scarcity and no made-up proof in any sample.

Sites Studied

Successful Sites In The Niche, Read For Structure

Read from each site's live page text in November 2026. These notes cover structure and messaging only. Borrow the technique, never the words, images or layout.

  • James Clear Creator and authorCredibility carried by specific numbers rather than adjectives. Navigation uses plain nouns: Books, Articles, Newsletter, Speaking, About. The About page routes onward to articles, books and a free email course, and the contact page says plainly what he declines.
  • Justin Welsh Creator and solo businessA newsletter page with no menu at all. One promise, the reader count stated twice, a stated reading time, 3 author blurbs and 6 reader comments. The thank-you page then routes new readers to the paid offers.
  • April Dunford ConsultantA one-line positioning headline and a single action, Contact, repeated 4 times down the page. Work is split into Consulting, Speaking and Books. Proof is 4 named testimonials with job titles plus a count of companies served.
  • Marie Forleo's B-School Course creatorThe weekly time commitment sits in the hero. 6 numbered modules each list what you will learn. The guarantee states its terms, testimonials carry a results-vary line, and a separate page says honestly who the program is not for.
