PERSUASION RULES
- Kahneman and Tversky, Anchoring: A $25,000 agency figure frames the 3 prices that follow.
- Dan Ariely, Decoy Effect: The Sprint and the Retainer make the Full Identity the natural centre.
- Kahneman and Tversky, Framing: Each price is paired with its duration: 1 day, 6 weeks, per month.
- Russell Brunson, Value Ladder: The Sprint fee is credited toward the Full Identity, so the ladder is easy to climb.
- John E. Kennedy; Claude Hopkins, Reason-Why And Specifics: Fixed prices come with a reason: you should know the cost before you decide.
- William Hick and Ray Hyman, Hick's Law: 3 options, each with a 1-line who-it-is-for.
- Google, Trust Signals: Payment terms, exclusions and what happens if it goes wrong are all in view.
