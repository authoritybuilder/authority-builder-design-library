The fine print, up front

In Every Project. And What Is Not.

Always included

A kick-off workshop, a single point of contact (me), source files you own outright and a hand-over call.

Never a surprise

If something is outside scope I will say so before I do it, with a fixed price for the extra.

Not included

Website build, copywriting and photography. I will introduce you to 3 people I trust for each.

Process

What Happens, And When

  1. Intro call

    30 minutes, free. We check the fit and pick a package.

  2. Kick-off

    A 2-hour workshop within 2 weeks of booking. You do the talking.

  3. Design

    You see work at set milestones, never a big reveal at the end.

  4. Hand-over

    Files, guidelines and a recorded walkthrough. Then 30 days of support.

Straight Answers On Price

Why fixed prices?

Because you should know the cost before you decide, and I should be rewarded for being efficient.

Can I start with a Sprint and upgrade?

Yes. The Sprint fee is credited in full if you move to a Full Identity within 60 days.

Do you offer a not-for-profit rate?

2 projects a year at 30% less. Ask on the intro call.

What if I do not like the direction?

You see 2 routes at the first milestone. If neither fits, we stop and you pay only for work done.

Pick A Package. Or Ask Which One.

A 30-minute intro call, free. You will leave knowing which option fits and when you could start.
