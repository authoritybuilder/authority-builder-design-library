007 · Signature Program Sales Page

A long-form sales page for a group coaching program. It walks the reader from recognised pain to a named method, then removes the risk of saying yes.

Open The Live SampleCopy The Build Prompt

Why This Page Works

  • It has no menu. A sales page has 1 job, so the only links are the join button and a footer with the policies.
  • It follows the order a careful buyer thinks in: is this me, why does it happen, what is the plan, what is inside, who are you, does it work, what does it cost, what if I am wrong.
  • The price arrives after the value and next to a guarantee, with both payment totals stated openly.
  • Scarcity is real and explained: 24 places so everyone gets call time, and 2 intakes a year.

Page Structure

  1. Hero: the reader's situation, the outcome and the timeframe. 1 button. No menu
  2. The problem, in the reader's own words: name it, show what it costs, then turn
  3. The named method: 3 stages that make the promise believable
  4. What is inside: 6 modules, each with outcomes, in an accordion that works without JavaScript
  5. Who it is for and who it is not for: protects the reader's right to say no
  6. The guide: why this coach, with specifics
  7. Proof wall: results first, in the client's words, with the results-vary note
  8. Price, shown after the value, with 2 ways to pay and the same total stated plainly
  9. Risk reversal with its terms stated honestly
  10. Objections answered in order of how often they are asked
  11. Closing call to action: the promise, the date and the same single button

Structured Data On This Page

  • Service. What you provide, who provides it and where.
  • BreadcrumbList. Shows where this page sits in the site, so the result displays a tidy path.
  • A sales page with no menu still needs a footer linking About, Contact, Privacy, Terms and Refunds. That is the version of the rule that fits.
  • Questions are written for readers. There is no FAQ markup: those rich results stopped appearing in May 2026.

Built Into Every Sample

  • A title that carries the brand name and a description that says who the page is for
  • 1 clear headline (H1) in the HTML itself, with every word of the page readable without JavaScript
  • Canonical address, Open Graph tags and a share image slot, all pointing at 1 version of the address
  • Structured data (JSON-LD) that matches the visible text
  • A trust footer on every page: About, Contact, Privacy, Terms and Refunds
  • Proof written as real text, so Google and AI tools can read and quote it
  • Mobile-first layout, tap targets of 48px or more, visible focus states and reduced-motion support
  • No image weight above the fold, so the main content can load inside Google's 2.5 second target

Left Out On Purpose

  • No llms.txt file: Google says its Search and AI features do not use it
  • No FAQ markup chasing a results-page bonus: FAQ rich results stopped appearing on 7 May 2026
  • No review stars on your own business: Google excludes self-serving reviews
  • No hidden text, keyword stuffing or pages rewritten into chunks "for AI"

Borrow This

  • Write the problem section as if reading the reader's diary, then end it with a reframe.
  • Give each module a deliverable, not a topic.
  • Print who it is not for.
  • State your guarantee's conditions in 1 sentence.

Study The Real Thing

  • Marie Forleo's B-School. Weekly time commitment in the hero, numbered modules with learning bullets, a guarantee with terms, and a separate page on who it is not for.

Read for structure and messaging. Borrow the technique, never the words, images or layout.

Build me a signature program sales page for my business, modelled on sample 007 in the Authority Builder Design Library.

ABOUT MY BUSINESS
- Business name and my name: [yours]
- Who I help and the result they get: [1 line]
- My main offer and its price: [yours]
- The 1 action I want a visitor to take on this page: [yours]
- Real proof I can use (names, results, numbers, press): [paste it here]

STRUCTURE, IN THIS ORDER
1. Hero: the reader's situation, the outcome and the timeframe. 1 button. No menu
2. The problem, in the reader's own words: name it, show what it costs, then turn
3. The named method: 3 stages that make the promise believable
4. What is inside: 6 modules, each with outcomes, in an accordion that works without JavaScript
5. Who it is for and who it is not for: protects the reader's right to say no
6. The guide: why this coach, with specifics
7. Proof wall: results first, in the client's words, with the results-vary note
8. Price, shown after the value, with 2 ways to pay and the same total stated plainly
9. Risk reversal with its terms stated honestly
10. Objections answered in order of how often they are asked
11. Closing call to action: the promise, the date and the same single button

DESIGN DIRECTION
Soft Organic. Sage, cream and clay with soft blob shapes. Calm, human and unhurried.
Fonts: DM Serif Display + Nunito Sans from Google Fonts. Keep 1 accent colour and use it only for the main action.
Mobile-first. Buttons at least 48px tall. Visible focus states. Respect reduced motion.

PERSUASION RULES
- Direct-response tradition (Dan Kennedy), Problem, Agitate, Solve: The opening names the problem, shows what another year costs, then offers the turn.
- Kahneman and Tversky, Loss Aversion: The pull quote frames inaction as a loss of the reason you started.
- Eugene Schwartz, The Unique Mechanism: Subtract, Systemise, Step Back is a named method with dated stages.
- Jack Brehm, Reactance: The fit check says plainly who the program is not for.
- Kahneman and Tversky, Anchoring: $1,350 a week of time is stated before the $3,600 price.
- Prelec and Loewenstein; Ariely, Pain Of Paying: A payment plan is offered, with its higher total shown, not hidden.
- Robert Cialdini, Scarcity: 24 places and a closing date, each with its reason.
- William Hick and Ray Hyman, Hick's Law: No navigation and a single repeated button.

SEARCH-READY RULES
- 1 clear H1. A title that includes my brand name. A description that says who the page is for.
- Every word in the HTML itself. Nothing that needs JavaScript to appear.
- JSON-LD structured data (Service, BreadcrumbList) that matches the visible text.
- Canonical tag, Open Graph tags and a share image, all on 1 version of my address.
- A footer linking About, Contact, Privacy, Terms and Refunds.
- No llms.txt, no FAQ rich-result markup, no review stars on my own business, no hidden text.
- No images above the fold heavier than they need to be. Aim for the main content to load in under 2.5 seconds.

HONESTY RULES
- Use only the proof I gave you. Do not invent testimonials, client names, numbers or press.
- Where I have not given you something, leave a clearly marked placeholder.
- No fake countdown timers and no invented scarcity. If I make an income or results claim, add a line that results are not typical.

OUTPUT
1 self-contained HTML file with inline CSS. Australian spelling. No em dashes.
