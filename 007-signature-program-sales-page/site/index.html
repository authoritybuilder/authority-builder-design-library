You Are Not Bad At Business. You Are Carrying All Of It.

You started this to have more freedom. Instead you answer client messages at dinner, quote jobs on Sunday night and have not taken 2 weeks off since you opened.

The work is good. The clients are happy. The numbers are fine. And you are tired in a way a long weekend does not fix.

It is not a discipline problem. It is a design problem. A business built around 1 person will always need that person. So we redesign it.

The method

The Steady Method: Subtract, Systemise, Step Back

  1. Subtract

    Weeks 1 to 4. We cut the offers, clients and tasks that cost more than they earn. Most owners free 8 hours a week here.

  2. Systemise

    Weeks 5 to 8. You document the 5 processes only you can do today, so someone else can do them next month.

  3. Step back

    Weeks 9 to 12. You hand over, test a 4-day week and set the numbers that tell you it is working.
