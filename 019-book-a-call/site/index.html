The 30 minutes

Exactly What Happens

  1. Minutes 0 to 10: where you are

    Revenue, team, capacity and what you have already tried.

  2. Minutes 10 to 20: where it hurts

    We find the 1 constraint doing the most damage.

  3. Minutes 20 to 30: what to do first

    3 priorities in order. If I can help with them, I will say how. If not, I will say who can.

Is this call for you?

Book It If. Hold Off If.

Hold off if you

  • Are pre-launch
  • Want marketing execution only
  • Need an answer today
  • Are not the decision-maker

Book a call if you

  • Own a clinic or practice with $500k+ revenue
  • Have at least 3 team members
  • Feel busy but not more profitable
  • Want an outside view within 2 weeks
