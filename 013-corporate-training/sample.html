Learning outcomes

By The End, Every Manager Can

01

Open the conversation

Start a difficult feedback conversation in 2 sentences, without a compliment sandwich.

02

Describe, not judge

Give feedback on behaviour and impact that the other person can act on.

03

Stay in the room

Respond to defensiveness, silence or tears without backing down or blowing up.

Half-day agenda

Four Hours, 70% Practice

  1. 9:00

    Why feedback fails

    A short, evidence-based opening. What the research says and what managers actually fear.

  2. 9:40

    The 2-sentence opener

    Demonstration, then 3 rounds of paired practice with real scenarios.

  3. 10:45

    Break

    15 minutes.

  4. 11:00

    When it gets hard

    Handling defensiveness. Practice in trios with an observer.

  5. 12:15

    Your next conversation

    Each manager plans 1 real conversation and books it before leaving.

  6. 12:45

    Close

    Commitments, resources and the 30-day follow-up.
