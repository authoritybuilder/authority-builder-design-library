# 013 · Corporate Training Page

**Group:** Offer Pages  
**For:** Consultant, Coach  
**Style:** Corporate Calm (Source Serif 4 + IBM Plex Sans)  
**Sample brand:** Brightline Learning (fictional)

A B2B training page written for the person who has to justify the spend: outcomes, an agenda, evaluation data, formats with prices and an easy enquiry.

## Why This Page Works

- It is written for a buyer who must justify the spend to someone else: outcomes, agenda, data and a price range are all forwardable.
- Outcomes are observable behaviours, which is how learning teams are asked to write them.
- Evaluation is measured at 30 days. That is the number a head of people is judged on.
- Indicative prices let a buyer check the budget before making contact, which shortens the sales cycle for both sides.

## The Psychology At Work

| Principle | Where you can see it |
|---|---|
| **Jobs To Be Done** (Clayton Christensen; Bob Moesta, Competing Against Luck (2016)) | The buyer's real job is to look credible to their executive. Every section hands them evidence. |
| **Authority** (Robert Cialdini, Influence (1984, revised 2021)) | A named facilitator with a doctorate, sector logos and evaluation data. |
| **Reason-Why And Specifics** (John E. Kennedy; Claude Hopkins, Scientific Advertising (1923)) | 70% practice, 94% within 7 days, 30-day follow-up: specifics in place of adjectives. |
| **Decoy Effect** (Dan Ariely, Predictably Irrational (2008)) | The keynote and the program frame the half-day as the sensible middle. |
| **Commitment And Consistency** (Robert Cialdini, Influence (1984, revised 2021)) | Managers book a real conversation before leaving the room, and the page says so. |
| **Fogg Behaviour Model** (BJ Fogg, Tiny Habits (2019)) | A 5-field enquiry and a 2-day turnaround make the first step easy. |
| **Trust Signals** (Google, Search Quality Rater Guidelines) | Supplier details, insurance and referees are offered before procurement has to ask. |

## Page Structure

1. Hero: the workshop, the audience, the format and the measurable outcome
2. Client strip: sectors and organisation types
3. Learning outcomes written as observable behaviours
4. A real agenda with timings: procurement and L&D want to see it
5. Evaluation data: before and after, from participant surveys
6. Formats with indicative prices, so a buyer can budget before enquiring
7. Testimonials from the buyer and from a participant
8. Enquiry form: 5 fields a proposal needs, with a promised turnaround

```text
DESIGN DIRECTION
Corporate Calm. White, slate navy and one teal. A serious serif, mono labels, hairline grids. Reassures a board.
Fonts: Source Serif 4 + IBM Plex Sans from Google Fonts. Keep 1 accent colour and use it only for the main action.
Mobile-first. Buttons at least 48px tall. Visible focus states. Respect reduced motion.

PERSUASION RULES
- Clayton Christensen; Bob Moesta, Jobs To Be Done: The buyer's real job is to look credible to their executive. Every section hands them evidence.
- Robert Cialdini, Authority: A named facilitator with a doctorate, sector logos and evaluation data.
- John E. Kennedy; Claude Hopkins, Reason-Why And Specifics: 70% practice, 94% within 7 days, 30-day follow-up: specifics in place of adjectives.
- Dan Ariely, Decoy Effect: The keynote and the program frame the half-day as the sensible middle.
- Robert Cialdini, Commitment And Consistency: Managers book a real conversation before leaving the room, and the page says so.
- BJ Fogg, Fogg Behaviour Model: A 5-field enquiry and a 2-day turnaround make the first step easy.
- Google, Trust Signals: Supplier details, insurance and referees are offered before procurement has to ask.

SEARCH-READY RULES
- 1 clear H1. A title that includes my brand name. A description that says who the page is for.
- Every word in the HTML itself. Nothing that needs JavaScript to appear.
- JSON-LD structured data (Service, Organization, BreadcrumbList) that matches the visible text.
- Canonical tag, Open Graph tags and a share image, all on 1 version of my address.
- Real About, Contact, Privacy, Terms and Refund pages, linked from the footer of every page.
- A robots.txt that allows search and AI-answer crawlers and lists the sitemap, and a sitemap.xml of every real page.
- A square PNG icon (192px) at a stable address.
- No llms.txt, no FAQ rich-result markup, no review stars on my own business, no hidden text.
- No images above the fold heavier than they need to be. Aim for the main content to load in under 2.5 seconds.

CONVERSION RULES
- The main button, 1 line of reassurance and 1 piece of proof sit together in the first screen.
- Ask for as little as possible in every form. State the reply time at the button.
- Print the price. Put the guarantee or refund terms beside every buy button.
- On phones, keep the main action in a bar at the bottom of the screen.

HONESTY RULES
- Use only the proof I gave you. Do not invent testimonials, client names, numbers or press.
- Where I have not given you something, leave a clearly marked placeholder.
- No fake countdown timers and no invented scarcity. If I make an income or results claim, add a line that results are not typical.

OUTPUT
1 self-contained HTML file with inline CSS. Australian spelling. No em dashes.
```
