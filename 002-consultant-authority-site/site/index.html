How we work

The Fee Architecture Process: 90 Days, 3 Deliverables

  1. Diagnose

    Weeks 1 to 3. We analyse 24 months of matters to find where margin is made and lost. You receive a 1-page margin map.

  2. Design

    Weeks 4 to 8. We build 3 pricing options per service line with your partners, tested against real past matters.

  3. Deploy

    Weeks 9 to 13. We train every fee-earner on the scoping conversation and stay beside you for the first 20 proposals.
