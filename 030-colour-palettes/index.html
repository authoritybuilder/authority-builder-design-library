030 · Colour Palettes, Contrast-Checked

10 palettes with every text and button pairing measured against the WCAG AA line of 4.5 to 1. If it is in here, it is readable.

How To Use A Palette

  • 1 accent colour, kept for the 1 action you want. The thing that looks different is the thing that gets noticed (the Von Restorff effect).
  • Most of the page is the page colour and the text colour. The alternate band and the contrast band give the scroll its rhythm.
  • Check contrast before you fall in love. Body text needs 4.5 to 1 against its background.
  • Pull your accent from something you already own: your logo, your book cover, the jacket in your headshot.
