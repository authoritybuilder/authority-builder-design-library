The 3 days

Unhurried, But Not Unstructured

  1. Friday

    Arrive and empty out

    3pm arrival. A long walk, dinner at 1 table and a single question to sleep on.

  2. Saturday am

    The honest review

    What worked, what did not and what you are avoiding. Small groups of 4.

  3. Saturday pm

    Nothing scheduled

    Swim, sleep or walk. Most of the thinking happens here.

  4. Sunday am

    The 1-page plan

    3 priorities, 3 things to stop and the first 30 days. Written by hand.

  5. Sunday pm

    Read it aloud

    Each founder reads their page to the group. Lunch, then home by 2pm.
