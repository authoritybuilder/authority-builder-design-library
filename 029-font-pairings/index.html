029 · Font Pairings For Personal Brands

10 pairings, every one free on Google Fonts. Each is used in at least 2 samples, so you can see it working on a real page before you commit.

How To Choose

  • Pick the display font for the feeling you want in the first second. Pick the body font for how it reads on a phone.
  • 2 families is plenty. A third only earns its place as a small accent, like a handwritten note or a mono label.
  • If the real font you love is paid, use the nearest free match and say so. Never pretend a substitute is the original.
  • Then stop changing it. A look you repeat is how people come to recognise you (Byron Sharp, How Brands Grow).
