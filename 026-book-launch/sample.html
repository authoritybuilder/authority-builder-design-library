The idea

The Book In A Paragraph

Most advice for consultants is audience advice: post more, publish more, grow the list. It works for some. For most, it produces a great deal of content and not many clients.

After advising 500 independent consultants, I noticed the fully booked ones had something else in common. They knew, by name, about 40 people who could send them work, and they looked after those relationships on purpose.

This book is about the 40. Who they are, how to find them and what to do each week.
