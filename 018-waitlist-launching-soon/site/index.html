A note from Celeste

Why I Am Building This

I ran my studio alone for 9 years before I found a mentor. The difference in the 3 years after was not talent. It was having someone to ask.

Atelier Year is the mentorship I wanted then: 12 months, a small group, real projects on the table. I am building it slowly, and I would like your help getting it right.

Celeste Marchetti

What We Can Tell You Now

What will it cost?

The founding rate will be between $4,000 and $5,000 for the year. The exact figure will be in the first email.

Who is it for?

Independent interior designers with at least 2 years in practice.

Does joining the waitlist commit me?

No. It only means you hear first.

What will you do with my email?

Send 2 or 3 emails about Atelier Year before April. It is not added to any other list.
