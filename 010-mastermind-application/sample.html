A note from Vivienne

At A Certain Size, The Advice Runs Out.

The books and podcasts that got you to your first million were written for people starting out. The people around you now either work for you or compete with you.

I built and sold a 60-person agency, and the most valuable thing I had in those years was a dinner table of 6 founders who told me the truth. The Long Table is that dinner, designed properly.

You do not need more information. You need a room where you are not the most experienced person in it.

Vivienne Ashcombe

The year

Four Parts, Deliberately Few

01

3 retreats

2 nights each, in February, June and October. Small, quiet venues within 2 hours of Sydney.

02

Monthly table

A 3-hour session on video. 1 member's business is the whole agenda.

03

A private adviser

6 one-to-one sessions with Vivienne across the year, booked when you need them.

04

The numbers book

Quarterly benchmarks shared among the 14, in confidence.

Who sits at the table

This Is A Fit For Very Few People

You will not, if you

  • Are pre-revenue or solo
  • Want a course or a curriculum
  • Would send a deputy in your place
  • Compete directly with a current member

You will fit if you

  • Own an agency with $1.5m to $8m in revenue
  • Lead a team of 8 to 60
  • Will share real numbers in confidence
  • Can attend all 3 retreats

How to apply

Three Steps, About 10 Days

  1. Apply

    8 questions, about 15 minutes. We ask for revenue range and team size, not financial statements.

  2. A conversation

    A 40-minute call with Vivienne. She will tell you on the call whether she thinks it is a fit.

  3. An invitation

    If we both agree, you receive an invitation and the member agreement within 3 days.
