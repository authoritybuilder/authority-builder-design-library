# 001 · Coach Homepage

**Group:** Home Bases  
**For:** Coach  
**Style:** Warm Editorial (Instrument Serif + Inter)  
**Sample brand:** Incognito & Field (fictional)

A personal-brand home page that names its audience in the first line, then offers 3 clear ways in: free, private and team.

## Why This Page Works

- The first line names the reader's exact situation, so the right person knows within 3 seconds that this page is for them.
- 3 doors match 3 levels of readiness: curious, ready and buying for a team. Nobody is forced to book a call to get started.
- The method has a name and a timeline. In a crowded coaching market that is easier to believe than a bigger promise.
- Every claim that matters is text, not an image: the numbers, the testimonials, the credentials. Google and AI tools can read and quote all of it.

## The Psychology At Work

| Principle | Where you can see it |

## Page Structure

1. Hero: who it is for, the promise, 1 main action and 1 proof number, all in the first screen
2. Proof strip: where clients come from, as plain text
3. Asset showcase: the thing itself, shown. Swap the files for your own video still, screenshots, slides or posts
4. Routing cards: 3 ways in, each with its commitment stated up front
5. About strip: a real story that opens with a failure, then credentials
6. Named method: 3 stages with a timeframe, so the promise feels believable
7. Testimonials as text, each led by a specific result
8. Picture band: a painted pause in the middle of the page that repeats the main button, straight after the proof
9. Content list: bylined answers that give Google and AI tools something to cite
10. Email capture: a small, free, weekly yes
11. Closing call to action that protects the right to say no

```text
DESIGN DIRECTION
Warm Editorial. Paper tones, a bookish serif, italic emphasis. Feels like a considered letter.
This site's own variation: Terracotta Letter. Sections rise a short way into place as they scroll in. It uses numbered section headers, cards lifted on soft shadows, arched section breaks, a paper grain, the method on a timeline. The picture band is a full-width painted band.
Fonts: Instrument Serif + Inter from Google Fonts. Keep 1 accent colour and use it only for the main action.
Mobile-first. Buttons at least 48px tall. Visible focus states. Respect reduced motion.

PERSUASION RULES
- Nielsen Norman Group, Attention Above The Fold: The promise, the named audience, the main button and a proof number all sit in the first screen.
- William Hick and Ray Hyman, Hick's Law: 3 doors, not 9. Each door states its commitment (5 minutes, 12 weeks, 6 weeks) so the choice is quick.

- Jack Brehm, Reactance: The closing note says a clear no is completely fine, which lowers the guard before the call.
- Google Search Central, Sitelinks Readiness: The menu uses 4 plain names, and the footer links 5 real pages. On your site, give each menu item its own page: distinct pages linked by plain names are what earn the extra links under a Google listing.

SEARCH-READY RULES
- 1 clear H1. A title that includes my brand name. A description that says who the page is for.
- Every word in the HTML itself. Nothing that needs JavaScript to appear.
- JSON-LD structured data (WebSite, Organization, Person) that matches the visible text.
- Canonical tag, Open Graph tags and a share image, all on 1 version of my address.
- Real About, Contact, Privacy, Terms and Refund pages, linked from the footer of every page.
- A robots.txt that allows search and AI-answer crawlers and lists the sitemap, and a sitemap.xml of every real page.
- A square PNG icon (192px) at a stable address.
- No llms.txt, no FAQ rich-result markup, no review stars on my own business, no hidden text.
- No images above the fold heavier than they need to be. Aim for the main content to load in under 2.5 seconds.

CONVERSION RULES
- The main button, 1 line of reassurance and 1 piece of proof sit together in the first screen.
- Ask for as little as possible in every form. State the reply time at the button.
- Print the price. Put the guarantee or refund terms beside every buy button.
- On phones, keep the main action in a bar at the bottom of the screen.

HONESTY RULES
- Use only the proof I gave you. Do not invent testimonials, client names, numbers or press.
- Where I have not given you something, leave a clearly marked placeholder.
- No fake countdown timers and no invented scarcity. If I make an income or results claim, add a line that results are not typical.

OUTPUT
1 self-contained HTML file with inline CSS. Australian spelling. No em dashes.
```
