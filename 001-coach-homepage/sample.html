Hi, I'm Imogen

I was the manager I now coach.

At 29 I was promoted over 3 of my friends. Within 6 months, 2 had resigned and I was at my desk until 9 most nights, redoing their work instead of leading it.

It took 2 years and 1 very honest mentor to learn what nobody teaches: leading is a different job, not a bigger version of the old one. I have spent the decade since turning that into a method.

  • 10 years leading people and culture teams
  • ICF-accredited coach (PCC)
  • Coaching Australia-wide, online

The method

The Hand-Over Method: 3 Shifts In 12 Weeks

  1. From doing to directing

    Weeks 1 to 4. We map what only you can do, then hand over the rest, with a script for every awkward conversation.

  2. From liked to trusted

    Weeks 5 to 8. You learn to give feedback people thank you for, and to hold a boundary without an apology.

  3. From busy to chosen

    Weeks 9 to 12. You rebuild your week around the 3 things your role is actually judged on.
