# 015 · Webinar Or Masterclass Registration

**Group:** List Builders And Launches  
**For:** Consultant, Coach  
**Style:** Dark Stage (Bebas Neue + Manrope)  
**Sample brand:** Carmen Ortiz Consulting (fictional)

A registration page for a live class. The date, the promise and the form sit together, then 3 beliefs the class will change.

## Why This Page Works

- Date, time, time zone and length are impossible to miss. Most webinar pages bury at least 1 of them.
- The class is sold as 3 beliefs it will change, which is more interesting than 3 topics it will cover.
- The run sheet openly says when the pitch happens and that you can leave before it. Honesty raises attendance.
- The deadline is real because the class is live at a real time. No evergreen countdown pretending to be live.

## The Psychology At Work

| Principle | Where you can see it |
|---|---|
| **The Perfect Webinar** (Russell Brunson, Expert Secrets (2017)) | 1 big promise, then 3 false beliefs to break, each tied to something concrete. |
| **Pre-Suasion** (Robert Cialdini, Pre-Suasion (2016)) | The registration page sets up the beliefs the class will go on to change. |
| **Scarcity** (Robert Cialdini, Influence (1984, revised 2021)) | A real date and a 48-hour replay. The limit is genuine. |
| **Reactance** (Jack Brehm, A Theory of Psychological Reactance (1966)) | The page says when the invitation comes and that leaving is fine. |
| **Commitment And Consistency** (Robert Cialdini, Influence (1984, revised 2021)) | Registering and adding a calendar invite are 2 small yeses before the class. |
| **Fresh-Start Effect** (Dai, Milkman and Riis, Management Science (2014)) | A lunchtime slot early in the month meets people when they are planning. |
| **Fogg Behaviour Model** (BJ Fogg, Tiny Habits (2019)) | 2 fields, a calendar invite and 1 reminder. |

## Page Structure

1. Hero: the promise, the date and time with time zone, and the form
2. What you will learn: 3 beliefs the class replaces, each with what you leave with
3. Run sheet: shows it is a class, not a 55-minute pitch
4. The host: experience and a number
5. Proof from past attendees of the free class
6. Practical questions: replay, cost, pitch
7. Closing call to action with the date repeated

## Search Card

- **Title:** Fully Booked Without Posting Daily | Free Masterclass With Carmen Ortiz
- **Description:** A free 60-minute live masterclass for independent consultants: the 3-part referral system that fills a calendar without daily content. Thursday 12 March, 12pm AEDT.
- **Structured data:** WebPage
- No Event markup here. Google's event rules centre on events with a real location, so check the current rules before marking up an online-only class.
- After the date passes, redirect this address to the next session or a waitlist. Do not leave a dead page indexed.

## Borrow This

- Put the time zone next to the time, and list 3 other cities in the questions.
- Frame what you teach as beliefs people will drop.
- Publish a minute-by-minute run sheet, including the pitch.
- Offer a replay with a real expiry.

SEARCH-READY RULES
- 1 clear H1. A title that includes my brand name. A description that says who the page is for.
- Every word in the HTML itself. Nothing that needs JavaScript to appear.
- JSON-LD structured data (WebPage) that matches the visible text.
- Canonical tag, Open Graph tags and a share image, all on 1 version of my address.
- A footer linking About, Contact, Privacy, Terms and Refunds.
- No llms.txt, no FAQ rich-result markup, no review stars on my own business, no hidden text.
- No images above the fold heavier than they need to be. Aim for the main content to load in under 2.5 seconds.

HONESTY RULES
- Use only the proof I gave you. Do not invent testimonials, client names, numbers or press.
- Where I have not given you something, leave a clearly marked placeholder.
- No fake countdown timers and no invented scarcity. If I make an income or results claim, add a line that results are not typical.

OUTPUT
1 self-contained HTML file with inline CSS. Australian spelling. No em dashes.
